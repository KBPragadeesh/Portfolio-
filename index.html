UI/UX Design Healthcare Design System WCAG AA
HCL Health care (HABIT)
1. Challenge & Objective Goal: Reduce drop-offs and user anxiety in multi-step booking flows across Healthcare and FinTech platforms. Strategy: Use clear, empathetic, and actionable microcopy to guide users from discovery to confirmation. 2. Microcopy Transformations Entry Point: Before: "Book Consultation" / "Select department below" After: "Find a Specialist" / “Search by symptom, specialty, or doctor name” Impact: Focuses on user intent over transactions. Mode & Selection: Copy: "In-Person" (Clinic visit) vs. "Video Call" (Secure in-app call) Impact: Sets clear expectations before picking dates. Reassurance Copy: Healthcare: "Your health details are strictly confidential and shared only with your doctor." FinTech: "We use bank-grade encryption to keep your financial data safe." Impact: Reduces hesitation at data-entry points. Confirmation & Fallbacks: Action: "Confirm & Book" + "Free cancellation up to 2 hours before." Confirmation: "You're all set!" with single-tap Add to Calendar and Reschedule options. 3. Key Takeaways & Impact Clarity: Replaced technical jargon with direct, conversational labels. Trust: Explicit privacy and policy microcopy decreased final-step exit rates. Efficiency: Clear calendar links and automated push reminders reduced client no-shows.

UX Writting HCLInternal Plugin (I Time Management) Design System WCAG AA
HCLInternal Plugin (I Time Management)
HCL Universal Orchestrator 1. The Problem & Solution Problem: HCL employees lost significant time manually re-entering repetitive task and time-tracking data into a complex dashboard week after week. Solution: Introduced a "Copy Previous Data" component to allow single-click duplication of prior week entries, cutting friction and data-entry overhead. 2. Microcopy Iterations & Evolution Button Label: Draft: "Data Import" → Too generic. Final: "Copy Previous Data" → Action-oriented and explicit. Contextual Subtext: Draft: "Copy data from previous periods" → Vague timeline. Final: "Previous week (07-Jul 2025)" → Direct date verification to prevent accidental overwrites. Confirmation Modal: Draft: "Are you sure? Previous unsaved data will be lost." → Generic and alarming. Final: "Are you sure you want to copy 125 entries from the previous week?" with CTAs [Yes, Copy] and [Review] → Clear, reassuring, and precise. 3. Impact 30% reduction in weekly data entry duration. 15% decrease in manual log errors. 95% user preference for specific entry-count modal copy during testing.
↑ Accessibility · ↓ 25% Dev Handoff · WCAG 2.1 AA

UI/UXHealthcareDesign System
FITZDO Healthcare Partner Portal
UI/UX Design · Healthcare · Design System · WCAG 2.1 AA

Concept

A unified digital ecosystem bridging Fitness, Wellness, and Healthcare Services — connecting healthcare partners, clinics, hospitals, and doctors with patients under the FITZDO ecosystem.

Goal

Responsive platform for hospital & partner management. Manage appointments, departments, medical records. Handle service listings, timings, staff details. Display patient health records, prescriptions, lab reports. Secure login, onboarding, profile management.

Design Philosophy

Color: #FF9800 FITZDO Orange breaks from "sterile blue" medical mold — signals energy, bridges fitness and healthcare.

Typography: Clean sans-serif headings ensure readability critical in high-stress medical environments.

Iconography: Department grid (Cardiology, Dental, etc.) uses simplified line-art for instant cognitive processing.

Results

  • WCAG 2.1 AA compliant throughout
  • Improved data visualization for medical professionals
  • Streamlined patient monitoring
  • Balanced medical reliability with high-energy brand elements
